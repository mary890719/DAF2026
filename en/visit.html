TRANSPORTATION

A. MRT–BUS CONNECTIONS

1. Tamsui–Xinyi Line Bus Connections (The venue is also approximately a 10-minute walk away)

  • From MRT Yuanshan Station, take Red 34 (weekends and public holidays only) to Lin An Tai Historical House (stop on Binjiang Street).
  • From MRT Yuanshan Station, take Red 50 to Xinsheng Park (stop on Minzu East Road).
  • From MRT Yuanshan Station, take bus 542 to Jilin Road Terminal (stop on Jilin Road).

2. Wenhu Line Bus Connection

  • From MRT Dazhi Station, take Brown 16 to Xinsheng Park (Lin An Tai) (stop on Songjiang Road near Binjiang Street).

3. Zhonghe–Xinlu Line Bus Connections

  • From MRT Zhongshan Elementary School Station, take bus 685 or 606 to Minzu East Road Intersection (stop on Xinsheng North Road near Minzu East Road; the ride is only two stops, and the venue is also approximately a 10-minute walk away).

B. DIRECT BUS SERVICES

  • Take bus 285, 665, Citizen Minibus 9, Red 34 (weekends and public holidays only), or Red 50 to Xinsheng Park (stop on Minzu East Road).
  • Take bus 72, 222, Red 34 (weekends and public holidays only), Brown 16, Citizen Minibus 9, or 527 to Xinsheng Park (Lin An Tai) (stop on Songjiang Road near Binjiang Street).
  • Take bus 542 or the Dunhua Main Line to Jilin Road Terminal (stop on Jilin Road).
  • Take bus 74 to Taipei Fish Market (stop on Minzu East Road).
