Under the theme Gray Autonomous Entity, the 2026 Taipei Digital Art Festival examines how digital art might respond to the rapid development of artificial intelligence by reconsidering emerging forms of agency, environmental relations, and artistic questions that remain difficult to define, continue to evolve, and can no longer be avoided.

As artificial intelligence becomes a new kind of entity—one that shapes artists’ everyday experience as well as their creative practice—how might digital art respond to a mode of existence that is neither simply a tool nor a medium?
